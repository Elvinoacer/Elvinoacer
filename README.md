## The story

A customer looking for medicine. A restaurant taking orders over an unreliable connection. A service provider trying to turn an inquiry into paid work. These are the kinds of problems that draw me to software.

I'm **Elvin Omondi Juma**, a **full-stack product engineer**, founding engineer at **GTSS**, and Computer Science student at **Moi University**, Kenya. I build across the whole product: the experience people see, the systems behind it, and the operations that keep it useful.

My work spans healthcare, local commerce, hospitality, and education. The common thread is practical: understand how people already work, find the friction, and build something they can depend on.

> Good software should make the next step clearer for the person using it.

## Selected work

### 01 / Afyvia

**Helping neighborhood pharmacies become easier to find and connect with.**

I'm building a pharmacy network where customers can discover nearby pharmacies, check listed medicine availability, explore services, and send prescription requests for pharmacist review. Pharmacies get a digital storefront and tools to manage customer relationships.

The engineering work centers on **tenant isolation, pharmacy onboarding, prescription review, subscriptions, and the customer journey**.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL`

[Explore Afyvia](https://afyvia.gtss.software/)

### 02 / JustInTime

**Connecting a local need to someone who can do the work.**

A services marketplace for Kenya, bringing discovery, proposals, bookings, conversations, and M-Pesa payment flows into one product for customers and service providers.

The engineering work centers on **multiple user roles, location-based discovery, booking state, real-time communication, and payment reconciliation**.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Prisma` `Socket.IO` `M-Pesa`

[Explore JustInTime](https://justintime.gtss.software/)

### 03 / Restaurant Manager

**Keeping service moving when the internet doesn't.**

An offline-first restaurant system designed around the local network, connecting guest orders, waiters, the kitchen, and administration without making every interaction depend on an internet connection.

The engineering work centers on **local state, kitchen events, order ownership, staff permissions, and payment verification**.

`Electron` `Node.js` `Socket.IO` `Local network`

**Start with the workflow.** Talk to the people doing the work. Map their decisions and handoffs before deciding what the product needs.

**Build the useful core.** Connect the interface to real permissions, data, and operations. Keep the design clear enough to change as the business learns.

**Treat failure as part of the product.** A delayed callback, a dropped connection, or a repeated click should have an intentional outcome. I pay attention to state transitions, retries, and recovery.

**Improve from feedback.** Put the product in front of the people it serves, watch where they get stuck, and make the next iteration more useful.

**Use AI with judgment.** I use agents to accelerate research, implementation, and review. I stay responsible for the architecture, validation, and the final result.
